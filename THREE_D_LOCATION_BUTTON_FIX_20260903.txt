AstroSight 3D map location-button fix - 2026-09-03

Confirmed cause
- The lower-right Current location / Subject / Tripod buttons updated React mapCenter only.
- mapCenter is consumed by the 2D map, but no Cesium camera move was performed for 3D.
- Therefore clicks could fire normally while the visible 3D map did not move.

Fix
- Reused existing src/cesium/camera.ts flyMapToTarget().
- Subject button: in 3D, fly Cesium camera to subject latitude/longitude/height.
- Tripod button: in 3D, fly Cesium camera to tripod latitude/longitude/height.
- Current location button: in 3D, resolve ground height and fly Cesium camera to current location; if elevation resolution fails, use the helper fallback.
- Existing setMapCenter behavior remains for 2D.

- Existing scripts/verify-3d-map-input-20260903.mjs: 6/6 PASS.
