AstroSight map UI simplification - 2026-08-30

Changes:
1. Removed the 2D map sky-dome/circular trajectory rendering, hourly trajectory dots/labels, Milky Way dome band, current celestial body circles/rays, observer-center circle, and tripod-subject auxiliary line from Map2DOverlay.
2. Kept only the celestial/tripod-search base lines that originate at the subject point. These lines are generated by buildTripodSearchBaseLines from the current celestial azimuth and extend from the subject toward the corresponding tripod-side direction.
3. Normal 2D-map tap now automatically places/moves the tripod pin at the tapped coordinate and resolves its ground elevation automatically.
4. Map drag and pinch/zoom remain available. A movement threshold distinguishes a tap (<6 px) from a pan so panning does not place a tripod pin.
5. Existing explicit subject/person/measurement placement modes continue to take precedence over automatic tripod placement.

- src/App.tsx
