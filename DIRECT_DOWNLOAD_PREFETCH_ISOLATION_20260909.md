# Direct download prefetch isolation — 2026-09-09

## Purpose
Remove a remaining source of stalls during the 360-bearing high-precision download without changing terrain precision.

## Confirmed bottleneck
After each authoritative `/api/gsi-elevation` result, `prefetchGsiDeviceTilesForSamples()` started extra `/api/gsi-dem-tile` downloads in the background. These requests were not governed by the foreground elevation-request scheduler. During continuous bearing processing they could overlap with the next high-precision elevation request and compete for browser/same-origin/Cloudflare network slots.

## Changes
- Replaced the per-call DEM tile prefetch worker pool with one global deduplicated queue.
- During `backfillBearingProfiles()` terrain generation, decoded DEM-tile persistence is paused and queued.
- After all foreground bearing terrain requests finish, queued tile persistence is resumed and drained before ancillary water/OSM finalization.
- Duplicate queued/in-flight tile fetches are suppressed by tile key.
- Background persistence concurrency is globally limited to 1 to avoid recreating foreground contention.
- No change to 1m maximum detail, DEM source priority, constrained bicubic interpolation, Karney geodesics, profile spacing, or 360-bearing coverage.

## Expected effect
Foreground high-precision elevation acquisition no longer competes with asynchronous decoded-tile persistence generated by the previous bearing. The total amount of required persisted data is not reduced by this change; the work is rescheduled so the latency-sensitive terrain phase can finish without self-induced network contention.
